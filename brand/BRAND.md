# Resuma Brand System

## Positioning

Resuma is the free intelligence layer for WhatsApp communities. It turns noisy group conversations into concise summaries, searchable memory, and useful signals without forcing members into another app.

## Promise

**O grupo fala. O Resuma entrega o que importa.**

## Symbol

The mark represents progressive reduction: three conversation lines become one clear signal. The separate dot is the useful outcome preserved after the noise is compressed.

The mark must never be described as artificial intelligence, a chatbot, or a messaging-app logo. It represents clarity created from collective conversation.

## Voice

- Clear before clever.
- Short, concrete sentences.
- Calm confidence; no exaggerated AI claims.
- Evidence over promises.
- Brazilian Portuguese on the commercial site; English in the public repository.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | `#0C1110` | Primary background and text |
| Warm Ivory | `#F3F1E8` | Reading surfaces and light backgrounds |
| Signal Lime | `#B7FF4A` | Action, state, and the preserved signal |
| Graphite 700 | `#2A3432` | Secondary surfaces |
| Graphite 500 | `#4A5653` | Muted text and borders |

Signal Lime is an accent, not a page background. Never pair it with generic purple or WhatsApp green.

## Typography

- Primary: Inter or a compatible modern grotesk.
- Secondary: JetBrains Mono for evidence, dates, commands, and technical labels.
- Use sentence case. Avoid all-caps paragraphs.

## Product status language

Available now: text summaries, sourced `!pergunta`, configured campaigns, commands, and group events.

In development: automatic podcast production, advanced moderation, multi-tenant onboarding, and the standalone administration panel.

Never present roadmap features as released.
